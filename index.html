Image Picker

A lively random picker that turns any list of students, participants or names into a suspenseful board game.

About this project

A single HTML file — no server, no sign-up, works offline in any browser. Photos and names are saved on the device itself, so the board stays ready between sessions and resume works instantly.

  • Classrooms & quizzes — pick students at random for answers or activities
  • Events & lucky draws — run a dramatic reveal on any big screen
  • Interactive displays & kiosks — touch-friendly, perfect for exhibitions and stalls
  • Offers, contests & activities — team selection, spot prizes, on-the-spot challenges

How to work

  1. Set up the board — choose how many students and what each card shows (image, name, or both).
  2. Add your students on the next screen — upload photos in bulk or load names from a CSV file.
  3. Press Random Pick and watch the suspense unfold while the spinner runs.
  4. Whichever number stops is revealed — and remembered for the next round.
